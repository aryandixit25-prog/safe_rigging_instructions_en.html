Safe Rigging Instructions

Do's ✔

  • Plan the lift: Always perform a risk assessment and have a detailed lifting plan before starting.
  • Inspect all equipment: Check all rigging gear (slings, shackles, hooks) for any damage before each use.
  • Use certified equipment: Only use equipment that is certified and has a clear working load limit.
  • Know the load: Determine the weight and center of gravity of the load before lifting.
  • Use taglines: Use at least two taglines to control the movement of the load and prevent spinning.
  • Wear proper PPE: Always wear appropriate personal protective equipment, such as a hard hat, safety gloves, and safety shoes.
  • Communicate clearly: Ensure clear communication with the crane operator using standard hand signals or radios.
  • Barricade the area: Create a clear barricaded area and ensure no unauthorized personnel are present.

Don'ts ✖

  • Don't overload: Never exceed the safe working load limit of any rigging component or the crane.
  • Don't work under a suspended load: Never stand or walk under a load that is being lifted.
  • Don't use damaged gear: Do not use any damaged, bent, or worn-out slings, ropes, or hooks.
  • Don't leave a suspended load unattended: Never walk away from or leave a load that is suspended in the air.
  • Don't shorten slings with knots: Never use knots, bolts, or other makeshift devices to shorten a sling.
  • Don't ride the load: It is strictly forbidden to ride on any lifted load.
  • Don't neglect weather conditions: Do not operate rigging equipment in adverse weather conditions like high winds.
  • Don't pull or push a moving load: Avoid pulling or pushing the load directly under the hoist.
